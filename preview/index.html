Plumbers, electricians, builders, HVAC techs and landscapers with small teams (1–10 staff) are brilliant at the actual trade — but most are still running their business on paper diaries, text messages and half-updated spreadsheets. Flowify is a done-for-you setup service — we implement Jobber (job scheduling & quoting), connect it to Xero (accounting), and add a 24/7 AI phone receptionist that answers every call, logs the job, and books it straight into the schedule. Clients pay a one-off setup fee, then a monthly retainer to keep it running and keep new jobs flowing in. The target is 20 retainer clients — a small, focused client base, not a huge company.

02 — THE NAME

Why "Flowify"?

"Flow" is the opposite of what these businesses feel day to day — bottlenecks, phone tag, quotes that sit for three days, invoices scrawled in a notebook until month-end. It's a promise that jobs, calls and payments move through the business smoothly instead of getting stuck.

"-ify" borrows the naming convention of Shopify, Spotify and the like — it signals "we make this happen for you," an active, modern, software-driven service rather than an old-school consultancy. And it lines up neatly with three things the business actually does: cash flow (faster payment via Xero), work flow (quote → schedule → job in Jobber), and communication flow (never missing a call, thanks to the AI receptionist).
